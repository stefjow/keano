About keano

What is this?

keano tracks NO₂ pollution in every ~36 km² hexagon on Earth and scores how well each one is cleaning up, month by month since May 2018. NO₂ comes mostly from combustion, which makes it a decent proxy for local air pollution.

The data

Monthly global NO₂ from Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI (Terrascope L3, 0.02° grid, quality-filtered), averaged into H3 resolution-6 hexagons with coverage weighting. The values are tropospheric column densities in µmol/m²: the total NO₂ in the air column above a hex, not what you would measure at the surface.

The base series: m

Everything is computed from m, the average NO₂ over the trailing 12 months. Averaging a full year at a time removes the seasonal cycle and smooths out retrieval noise. Since m only uses past months, scores are never revised: a new month appends, closed months stay as they were.

The layers

Trend is the annualized change of m since the cell's first full year, YoY compares m to its value one year earlier, and NO₂ shows the current level of m. Credit is explained below.

Credits

A cell earns credit when m drops below its own historical minimum. The baseline B is the lowest m in the window from 5 years ago to 1 year ago, i.e. the best year that ended at least a year ago, and m has to undercut it by more than a 2% noise margin. Credit is the relative undercut (B − m)/B, so a clean cell and a dirty cell earn the same for the same percentage improvement. Records expire after five years, which means every cell periodically has something new to earn.

Two extra rules apply. Only cells with m ≥ 30 µmol/m² are scored, because percent changes mean nothing at background noise level. And since NO₂ travels tens of kilometres, a record low can simply be the neighbour's plume blowing somewhere else this year, so full credit is only paid when the surrounding ~1,770 km² region is also below its own baseline. Isolated implausible records are zeroed.

The site

The whole thing is static files, no backend. Metrics are packed into binary planes, and the per-hex trend chart is fetched with a ~200-byte HTTP range request on hover. Built with MapLibre GL and H3. Clicking a hex's ⬡ code copies a link that jumps straight to it.
